# Invariants

## Face

Oval face, almond dark eyes with moderate spacing, straight serious brows, low-to-medium brow distance, narrow nose bridge, medium-width natural lips, gently tapering jaw, small mole under left eye when visible.

## Hair

Dark brown shoulder-length hair with slight natural wave, practical styling.

## Body And Proportions

Medium height, lean build, narrow shoulders slightly wider than hips, slightly long torso, long arms, narrow vertical silhouette, reserved posture.

## Age Presentation

Adult late twenties to early thirties. No teen, childlike, or student-age presentation.

## Presence

Quiet, observant, self-contained, restrained.
