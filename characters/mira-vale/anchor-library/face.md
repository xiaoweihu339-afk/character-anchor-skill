# Face

- Oval face with a softly defined jaw.
- Calm almond-shaped dark eyes with natural spacing.
- Moderate eye spacing, not wide-eyed or doll-like.
- Straight brows with a slight serious cast.
- Low-to-medium brow distance that keeps her expression focused rather than surprised.
- Soft cheekbones without sharp fashion-model contouring.
- Narrow nose bridge and natural nose tip.
- Medium-width mouth with natural lips, usually relaxed rather than smiling broadly.
- Jaw tapers gently into a small but not pointed chin.
- Small mole under the left eye when shot distance allows.
- Avoid generic beauty-face smoothing, oversized eyes, heavy glamour makeup, or teen-like softness.
