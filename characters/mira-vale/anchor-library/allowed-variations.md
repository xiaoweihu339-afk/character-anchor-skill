# Allowed Variations

## Outfit

Functional coats, shirts, trousers, boots, rain gear, archive gloves, satchels, and field research layers.

## Pose

Standing, walking, reading, listening, turning, holding a notebook, studying evidence.

Holding the notebook is allowed but not required; it should not appear in every shot unless the scene calls for it.

## Expression

Neutral, concerned, focused, tired, faint smile, restrained fear, quiet determination.

## Scene

Archive rooms, rain streets, train stations, old houses, libraries, field research sites, backstage corridors.

## Camera And Lighting

Close-up, medium shot, profile, over-the-shoulder, natural light, rain reflections, low practical lamps.

## Requires User Confirmation

Major age shift, body redesign, glamorous styling, comedic exaggeration, or non-realistic cartoon style.
