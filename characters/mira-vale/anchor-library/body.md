# Body

- Medium height.
- Lean build with narrow shoulders and long arms.
- Shoulders are only slightly wider than the hips, creating a narrow vertical silhouette.
- Slightly long torso with straight, practical posture rather than exaggerated curves.
- Reserved posture, often slightly inward or angled as if listening.
- Weight often rests subtly on one leg, with arms close to the body.
- Compact movement rather than exaggerated heroic stance.
- Practical physical presence, not athletic, voluptuous, doll-like, extremely tall, or runway-model thin.
