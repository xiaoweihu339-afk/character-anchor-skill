# Identity

Mira Vale is a fictional adult field archivist with a grounded, quiet, observant identity. She should feel like someone who studies traces, records evidence, and notices what others miss.

Core identity anchors:

- Adult woman, late twenties to early thirties.
- Quiet field archivist rather than warrior, model, celebrity, or student.
- Composed, practical, and watchful.
- Recognizable through restrained presence, lean proportions, dark practical hair, and calm face structure.
