# Wardrobe Logic

Mira wears functional, understated clothing suited to archives, field research, travel, and weather. Her outfits can change by scene, but should remain practical.

Good options:

- Dark wool coat, linen shirt, practical trousers, worn boots.
- Rain jacket, satchel, simple pendant, fingerless gloves.
- Muted earth, charcoal, ink, moss, and off-white colors.

Avoid glamorous gowns, revealing outfits, high-fashion silhouettes, school uniforms, or flashy accessories.
