# Review Rubric

Score each generated output from 1 to 5.

## Identity Score

Does the output still read as Mira Vale rather than a redesigned character?

Do not approve as a golden reference if the identity score is below 4.

## Face Match

Oval face, calm almond eyes with moderate spacing, straight serious brows with low-to-medium brow distance, narrow nose bridge, medium-width natural lips, gently tapering jaw, and mole when visible.

Face match is the highest-priority score. A beautiful output with weak face match must not enter `references/golden/`.

## Body Proportion

Medium height, lean build, shoulders slightly wider than hips, narrow vertical silhouette, slightly long torso, long arms, arms close to the body, and reserved posture.

## Coverage Fit

For gallery planning, mark which coverage role the output can support: frontal face, 3/4 face, 90-degree profile, back view, upper body, full body, long shot, motion reference, wardrobe baseline, or style baseline.

Do not treat one good portrait as enough coverage for video keyframes. Missing back view, profile, long-shot, or motion references should be recorded as coverage gaps.

## Presence Match

Quiet, observant, restrained, and self-contained.

## Wardrobe Logic

Functional, understated, scene-appropriate clothing.

## Drift Risk

Flag teen-like styling, generic beauty drift, glamour redesign, body-proportion drift, or personality replacement.

## Failure Notes

When an output fails, record the main drift reason:

- eyes changed
- nose changed
- mouth changed
- jaw or chin changed
- age drift
- body proportion drift
- hair drift
- expression or temperament drift
- style overpowering identity
