# Mira Vale Anchor Card

## Identity Summary

Mira Vale is a fictional adult character designed for AI video and cinematic image workflows. She reads as a quiet field archivist: observant, composed, and slightly guarded, with a practical visual identity rather than a glamour-first one.

## Age Presentation

Adult, late twenties to early thirties. Never present her as teen, student-age, childlike, or age-ambiguous.

## Face Anchors

Oval face with a softly defined jaw, calm almond-shaped dark eyes, straight brows with a slight downward seriousness, a narrow nose bridge, natural lips, and a composed resting expression. Keep her face recognizable without turning it into a generic beauty portrait.

## Hair Anchors

Dark brown shoulder-length hair, usually loosely tied back or tucked behind one ear. Slight natural wave, practical rather than salon-styled.

## Body And Proportion Anchors

Medium height, lean build, narrow shoulders, long arms, reserved posture, and compact movement. She should not become athletic, curvy, doll-like, extremely tall, or runway-model thin.

## Distinctive Details

Permanent identity mark: small mole under the left eye when shot distance allows.

Optional recurring props and accessories: weathered leather notebook and thin brass pendant. These help continuity, but they are not required in every shot.

## Temperament And Expression Range

Quiet, observant, self-contained, and precise. She can smile, worry, or act decisively, but her emotions stay restrained and interior.

## Visual Style Baseline

Cinematic realism, natural light, grounded color, documentary texture, subtle film grain, and practical costume design.

## Allowed Variations

Scene, weather, camera angle, lighting, practical outfits, expression, and pose may vary. Clothing should remain functional and understated.

## Forbidden Changes

Do not change her age presentation, face structure, body proportions, dark hair, restrained presence, or grounded styling. Avoid glamorous fashion-editorial redesigns.

## Golden References

No image references in the MVP demo. This character is anchored through text-only identity rules.

## Common Drift Risks

Generic pretty face, oversized eyes, teen-like styling, high-fashion wardrobe, exaggerated hourglass body, overly cheerful expression, or action-hero posture.

## Current Best Reusable Prompt

Mira Vale, fictional adult field archivist, oval face, calm almond dark eyes, softly defined jaw, dark brown shoulder-length practical hair, lean medium-height build with reserved posture, quiet observant presence, understated functional clothing, small mole under left eye, weathered notebook, cinematic realism, natural light, grounded color, subtle film grain.
